## Defects and fixes

### `RF-GAL-001` — focus indicator was too thin

- **Observed:** Forced Button and Link focus specimens computed a one-pixel solid outline on light surfaces. The inverse Button added a four-pixel teal-100 halo, but the light Button and Link indicators did not provide the WCAG 2.2 Focus Appearance minimum equivalent.
- **Expected:** Button and Link focus indicators use a token-backed outline at least two CSS pixels thick on every approved light and inverse surface.
- **Affected files:** `tokens/source/effects.json`, `components/Button/Button.css`, `components/Link/Link.css`, and `examples/component-gallery/src/gallery.css`.
- **Root cause:** Interactive focus styling reused the structural `border.width.hairline` token instead of defining a focus-specific width.
- **Regression:** `scripts/tests/focus-appearance.test.ts` requires a focus-width dimension of at least 2px and requires Button, Link, inverse Link, and forced gallery specimens to consume it.
- **Fix:** Added `border.width.focus` at `2px`, regenerated artifacts and the documentation snapshot, and replaced hairline focus outlines with the new token. The inverse halo remains additive.
- **Status:** Fixed; focused regression and full validation pass.
